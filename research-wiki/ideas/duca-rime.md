# Idea: DUCA-RIME

## State

`implemented`, `tested`, `redeployment_pending`,
`not_yet_empirically_supported`

## One-sentence method

DUCA-RIME uses cheap inference-visible temporal evidence to predict
classification/boundary utility and pair risk, converts it into a constrained
monotone acquisition density and a frozen-price budget decision, then executes
only the exact selected frames before an otherwise registered TAD detector.

## Novelty candidate

The publishable unit is not generic token pruning. It is the conjunction of:

1. detector-utility and pair-risk supervision derived without final-set
   leakage;
2. risk-aware dynamic pre-backbone compute allocation;
3. bounded monotone physical-time exact-K decoding;
4. matched realized-K causal controls and full-stack cost evidence;
5. high-IoU/short-action/pair-support claim gates.

## Competition and AdapTok boundary

The direct AdapTok-style arm is retained as a named causal comparator. DUCA-RIME
must not claim novelty from adaptive token count alone. Its differentiating
claim, if supported, is task-aware offline TAD acquisition with physical-time
coverage, localization-risk protection, and exact realized-cost controls.

## Falsifier

If RIME fails to beat `U-same-K`, then dynamic K alone is not the contribution.
If it fails to beat `U-fixed`, task-aware positions are not useful at the
registered cost. If high-IoU or short-action gates fail, risk protection is
insufficient. Any of these outcomes blocks the broad paper claim.
