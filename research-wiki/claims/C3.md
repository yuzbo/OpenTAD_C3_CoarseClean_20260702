# C3: DUCA fixed-K 优于 matched uniform/random

## Statement

DUCA fixed-384/256/128 在相同 detector、commit 和实际成本下优于 exact-uniform、periodic 和 random。

## Current evidence

70aa fixed-384 已完成，最终 Avg-mAP 58.39，mAP@0.7 为 34.53；可审计的历史分离 lattice 最佳值为 63.18，PAction 最佳值为 61.02，但它们使用 218 step/epoch，而 DUCA 只有 99 step/epoch。用户记录的 uniform 约 65 尚未定位到与 70aa 同 commit、同 loader、同 effective-K 的成功日志。当前结果既不支持 DUCA 优于 uniform，也不能在协议未匹配时证明联合训练本身必然更差。

The corrected exposure-132 seed-0 suite at immutable commit `1642f26`
provides the first matched terminal-EMA comparison: exact-uniform 63.8594,
transition-beta0 64.2755, and CellCF 64.0610 Avg-mAP. Transition-beta0 gains
only 0.4161 points over uniform; CellCF is 0.2145 below transition-beta0.
This is too small and too narrow to establish robust fixed-K superiority, and
the registered CellCF utility extension does not improve the transition-only
arm. C3 remains `unproven`.

The completed `8ebdd2a` training-side ceiling cannot decide C3 through frozen
loss. It shows worse deploy-score geometry and worse frozen candidate loss,
but no decoded/NMS mAP. The next admissible result is a single-use hash-bound
same-checkpoint replay of exact uniform versus deploy-visible transition
selection; privileged GT selection is excluded from the deployable comparison.

## Evidence still required

当前 one-per-cell CellCF 已从主方法终止，不能再作为 C3 的优先申诉。下一步先做
allocation-family exact ceiling、physical-time geometry 和成本门禁；只有新的
coverage-scaffold + global residual family 明确增加边界 allocation freedom、释放背景
quota 且存在 break-even 可能，才允许 same-commit fixed-384 小规模训练。通过后再做
多 seed、置信区间和 random/dense 扩展。
