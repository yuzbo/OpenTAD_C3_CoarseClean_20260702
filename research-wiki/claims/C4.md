# C4: 下游 detector 梯度改善 selector

## Statement

真实 TAD detector loss 经 structured bridge 提供的梯度能改善 hard frame replacement utility 和 mAP。

## Current evidence

已有 nonzero gradient 和 frame-selector optimizer coverage tests；hard/soft policy 已核实属于
同一 exact-K/max-gap 可行族。这仍只证明 constrained connectivity，不证明 detector gradient
与 hard replacement utility 同向；full-model optimizer exact coverage 也未闭环。

The completed CellCF suite does not test this claim because
`detector_gradient_mode=none`: hard local alternatives are evaluated under
no-grad and distilled as detached utility. CellCF reaches 64.0610 versus
64.2755 for transition-beta0, so this detached utility also shows no seed-0
Avg-mAP gain. Direct detector-gradient benefit remains untested and C4 remains
`unproven`.

## Evidence still required

protected/no-bridge ablation、finite-difference one-swap alignment、full-model optimizer
coverage 和最终 mAP。

## 2026-07-20 protected-E2E gate diagnostic

Post-prompt commits through `b3222af` connected a protected detector-only
gradient to the selector scorer under the real VideoMAE-S/AdaTAD stack.
Job `1176948` also observed the intended zero gradients in the protected
ASFormer/action-head groups, and the rho arm reached only the final ASFormer
layer. This advances connectivity and source ownership for that candidate.

It does not support C4. The implemented local transport is not the Gibbs
marginal of the same physical exact-K hard feasible set, selected-axis GT
remap remains active, and P3 stopped before measuring alignment. The
superseding design requires a physical-DAG finite-difference gate and a
strictly matched terminal-EMA mAP comparison.

## 2026-07-16 mechanism-scope correction

The `7525efb` Round-1 audit confirms `direct_detector_gradient=false`. Its
teacher evaluates discrete hard swaps under `no_grad` and supervises the
scorer with detached selection-policy utility. The signed score-space
proximal identity proves a local surrogate direction only. Therefore this
candidate does not directly test C4 as currently worded; a positive result
would support a separate detached detector-derived policy-utility claim, not
direct detector-loss backpropagation through hard selection.

The Round-2 Local-cell proposal retains that detached mechanism and therefore
must not label its `local utility > beta0` comparison as C4 without a formal
claim rewrite. C4 remains unproven and is not the direct result target of the
designed CellCF route.

The implemented CellCF route still sets `detector_gradient_mode=none`. Its
official detector evaluates hard local flips under no-grad and supplies a
detached signed policy-utility target. It does not revive or test direct-
gradient C4; any positive result requires a separately worded claim.

## 2026-07-12 audit correction

The running beta=0/beta=0.25 pair starts from a defective alpha=0 reference,
so it is not a clean test of the intended continuous curriculum. Nonzero real
detector gradients still establish connectivity only. A corrected matched pair
plus one-swap gradient alignment is required before any utility claim.

## Completed legacy diagnostic

Beta0.25 trailed beta0 by 0.79 best Avg-mAP. Its best-checkpoint mAP@0.7 was
0.31 higher but final mAP@0.7 was 0.52 lower. Under the invalid homotopy this
does not support a detector-gradient benefit. The strict result-to-claim
verdict is `no` with high confidence; this is failure to support C4, not a
protocol-valid refutation of the broader bridge hypothesis.

## 2026-07-13 route distinction

The external audit recommends replacing direct raw-pixel gradient bridging with
train-only feasible-swap counterfactual utility distillation. That proposal does
not support this claim: it changes the mechanism from detector-gradient
backpropagation to a stop-gradient detector-derived ranking target. If adopted,
it requires a separate claim and cannot be reported as validation of C4.

## 2026-07-15 exact-suite invalidation

Job `1164703` has already missed four successful updates by the end of epoch
24, while the current counterfactual gate only checks finite sign agreement and
Spearman values rather than positive directional alignment. It therefore
cannot support C4 even if it later produces a competitive mAP. C4 remains
`unproven`; a replay-safe matched suite and a preregistered positive one-swap
alignment gate are both required.
