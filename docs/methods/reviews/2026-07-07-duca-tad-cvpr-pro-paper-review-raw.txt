## 1. Overall Verdict on Completed Sections

**等级：Needs major rewrite.**

当前前半部分**不是 CVPR submission-ready**。它比普通 proposal 更接近论文雏形，因为任务领域、部署约束、稀疏 ledger、original-time decoding 这些核心概念已经出现；但它仍然明显像“系统路线说明 + 实验计划”，还不像一篇可信的 CVPR 方法论文。Abstract 直接写 “Experiments will evaluate ...”，Method 大量使用 “can / may / optionally / current implementation family”，Related Work 只有少量代表性 TAD 引用，关键术语如 **utility-calibrated / value-transport / TrueTime / detector utility / risk / redundancy** 没有被严格定义为可复现算法对象。稿件目前把很多尚未锁死或尚未验证的路线同时写进论文，导致 reviewer 会怀疑：这到底是一个具体方法，还是一个研究框架清单。

最严重的问题不是缺结果，而是**方法主张和实现合同没有收敛**。你声称 DUCA-TAD 是 dynamic、utility-calibrated、detector-aware、TrueTime、value-transport、deployable，但目前每个词都只有方向性描述，没有足够的数学定义、训练目标、推理算法、接口约束和不可变实验协议来防止 reviewer 把它看成“actionness top-k + gap repair + metadata bookkeeping”的包装。Problem Formulation 和 Method 已经列出 selector、动态预算、ledger、TrueTime grid 等对象，但公式和算法仍是占位型，尚不足以支撑 CVPR 方法章节的可复现性。

我的判断：**概念方向可以保留，但必须大改叙事和方法收敛方式；不能直接给外部 reviewer。**

## 2. Top 10 Critical Issues

### 1. Severity: Critical

**Location:** Abstract, last sentence.
**Problem:** “Experiments will evaluate whether ...” 是典型 proposal 语气，不是论文语气。它承认核心主张尚未被验证，而且 abstract 前面已经使用了 “learns”, “utility-calibrated”, “preserves” 等偏完成式 claim。
**Why it hurts CVPR review:** Reviewer 会立即把稿件定位成未完成研究，而不是未填结果的完整论文草稿。即使你要求不因实验空缺拒稿，正式 CVPR 写作中这句话也会严重伤害可信度。
**Concrete fix:** 改成方法性摘要，不用未来时。结果未完成时可以写成“we evaluate using a protocol that ...”，最终版必须替换成真实数字。当前可改为：
“DUCA-TAD is evaluated under fixed- and dynamic-budget acquisition protocols that report high-tIoU mAP, selected-frame compute, browser overhead, and leakage diagnostics.”

### 2. Severity: Critical

**Location:** Method 4.2–4.4.
**Problem:** 方法没有收敛到一个唯一实现。文本同时允许 low-resolution pixels、compressed descriptors、motion deltas、coarse actionness probe；utility target 又可以是 coverage、boundary proximity、detector responsibility、classification loss、regression loss、counterfactual utility；dynamic budget 可以是 bucket、threshold 或 constrained count。
**Why it hurts CVPR review:** Reviewer 无法判断你到底实现了什么，也无法复现。CVPR 方法可以有 variants，但主方法必须是一个明确算法，variants 应放到 ablation，而不是 Method 主体里用 “can / may” 描述。
**Concrete fix:** 固定主线。例如：
“Primary DUCA uses a low-cost p-action TCN browser with inputs ([p, \Delta p, |\Delta p|, H(p), u(p), t/T, m]), predicts value (g_t), boundary (b_t), redundancy (r_t), and budget bucket (K), and decodes (S) by gap-constrained top-K. Other descriptor sources are ablations.”
把 “can add / may combine / optionally” 全部降级为 appendix 或 ablation。

### 3. Severity: Critical

**Location:** Method 4.5–4.6 and Figure 1.
**Problem:** “TrueTime interface” 与 “unchanged detector” 存在逻辑冲突。4.5 写 TrueTime grid 被 assignment、feature downsampling、area-time heads、decoding、NMS 使用；4.6 又写 AdaTAD instantiation leaves VideoMAE adapter, projection, and ActionFormer-style head unchanged，Figure 1 也写 “unchanged detector”。
**Why it hurts CVPR review:** 这是方法正确性红线。如果 detector 真不读 irregular metadata，那 TrueTime 只是记录而不是方法；如果 assignment/decode/NMS 读 metadata，那 detector/head/postprocess 就不是 unchanged。这个矛盾会让 reviewer 怀疑实验协议和代码合同。
**Concrete fix:** 二选一，不能两边都要：

* 若使用原生 TrueTime head：写成 “we keep the detector architecture but replace temporal coordinate operators with ledger-aware original-time assignment, decoding, and NMS.”
* 若使用 unchanged dense head：不要声称 TrueTime assignment；写成 “selected-axis training with final dense-time remapping”，并把 TrueTime 作为 future/variant。
  建议用前者，但必须给出 decode 公式、NMS 坐标轴和 assignment target 的具体定义。

### 4. Severity: Critical

**Location:** Problem Formulation Eq. 2 and Method 4.4.
**Problem:** “value-transport” 没有被定义为 transport。Eq. 2 中 (m_t) 被称为 transport mass，但没有 source slots、target frames、cost matrix、mass conservation、transport plan 或 optimization；Method 实际写的是 score ranking + gap constraints。
**Why it hurts CVPR review:** “Value-transport” 听起来像 optimal transport 或 structured allocation，但正文只实现 constrained top-K。术语过强会被认为是营销。
**Concrete fix:** 如果你没有真正的 transport problem，把名字改成 **constrained utility ledger decoding**。如果保留 value-transport，必须定义：
[
P^*=\arg\min_{P\in\Pi(a,b)} \sum_{u,t} P_{u,t} C(u,t)-\alpha P_{u,t} q_t
]
其中 (u) 是 budget slot，(t) 是 dense time，(\Pi) 是单调/覆盖约束；然后 (m_t=\sum_u P^*_{u,t})。否则 (m_t) 不应出现在主公式里。

### 5. Severity: Critical

**Location:** Method 4.3.
**Problem:** “detector utility” 是整篇论文最核心的 claim，但目前只是列举可能来源：classification loss、regression loss、responsibility、counterfactual frame utility 等。没有说明 utility target (y_t) 如何计算、是否需要 dense teacher、是否离线、是否 per-frame、是否对 train/val split 安全。
**Why it hurts CVPR review:** 如果 detector utility 不清楚，DUCA-TAD 就退化成 actionness / boundary heuristic。Reviewer 会问：utility-calibrated 到底 calibration 了什么？与 detector loss 的关系在哪里？
**Concrete fix:** 在 Method 中加入一个严格小节：
“Training-time utility target construction.”
至少定义一种主 target，例如：
[
y_t = \max_j \exp(-d(t,\partial b_j)/\sigma_b) + \gamma \cdot \mathrm{resp}*t^{cls/reg}
]
或
[
y_t = \Delta \mathcal{L}*{det}(V\setminus t)-\mathcal{L}_{det}(V)
]
如果 counterfactual 太贵，就不要写成主方法。并明确：utility targets are computed only on training videos by a frozen dense teacher and are never computed for validation/test ledgers.

### 8. Severity: Major

**Location:** Experiments skeleton 5.1–5.4.
**Problem:** 实验框架方向正确，但 baseline list 不足，5.2 基本为空，compute 只说 selected frame count、FLOPs、memory、latency，没有强制包括 browser cost、ledger generation cost、retraining cost、per-window dynamic-K distribution。

### 10. Severity: Major

**Location:** Problem Formulation 3.2 and Experiments 5.4.
**Problem:** 动态预算 (K=B_\eta(\cdot)) 只被描述为 per-window budget，没有定义目标约束。你说 dynamic budget must be auditable，但没有定义 global budget、expected budget、latency bound、max K、min K、short-window behavior、matched controls。

[
\max_{\theta,\eta} \mathrm{mAP}*{0.3:0.7}(D(A*{\theta,\eta}(V)))

\mathbb{E}[K]\le \bar K,; K\in[K_{\min},K_{\max}],; C_{total}\le C_0.

实验必须有 “DUCA dynamic vs DUCA fixed at same mean K” 和 “DUCA dynamic vs uniform fixed at same total latency”。

### Related Work

**是否达到 CVPR 标准：** 不达标。
当前 Related Work 是一个基础 TAD 背景段落，而不是 CVPR 级定位。只覆盖少数 detector 和 backbone 引用，缺少最近 efficient TAD、adaptive frame selection、video token/frame selection、model compression、benchmark standardization。

**最大逻辑问题：** 没有明确最近邻方法。Reviewer 会问：DUCA-TAD 与 efficient TAD compression、adaptive video sampling、long-video keyframe selection、token pruning 的差异是什么？

### Problem Formulation

**是否达到 CVPR 标准：** 部分达到。

## 5. Language and Style Surgery

下面是需要直接替换的句子或表达。

1. **Original:** “Experiments will evaluate whether utility-calibrated sparse acquisition reduces redundant temporal computation...”
   **Replace:** “We evaluate sparse acquisition under fixed- and dynamic-budget protocols that jointly report detection accuracy, high-tIoU localization, selected-frame compute, browser overhead, and leakage diagnostics.”

2. **Original:** “learns which temporal observations should be delivered”
   **Replace:** “predicts deployable acquisition scores for candidate temporal observations.”

3. **Original:** “The core idea is to train a low-cost browser to estimate deployable acquisition value and then transport that value...”
   **Replace:** “DUCA-TAD uses a low-cost temporal browser to score candidate observations and a constrained decoder to convert these scores into an auditable sparse ledger.”

4. **Original:** “value-transport module”
   **Replace:** “constrained utility decoder” unless you formally define transport.

5. **Original:** “current implementation family”
   **Replace:** “In our implementation” or delete variants.

6. **Original:** “A richer browser can add start, end, boundary, risk, uncertainty, redundancy, and role heads.”
   **Replace:** “The browser predicts value, boundary, uncertainty, and redundancy-proxy heads; ablations remove each head.”

7. **Original:** “Their purpose is not to solve TAD, but to estimate which observations are worth delivering to the detector.”
   **Replace:** “These heads are trained to predict detector-relevant acquisition value, not final action categories or segments.”

8. **Original:** “DUCA-TAD uses train-only utility to teach the browser what matters for TAD.”
   **Replace:** “During acquisition training, frozen-detector utility targets computed on the training split supervise the browser.”

9. **Original:** “A practical training objective is...”

## 6. Novelty and Positioning Risk

当前前半部分**还不足以让 reviewer 确信这不是普通 adaptive frame sampling**。它有潜力，但还没有把差异钉死。

Reviewer 最可能的攻击是：

“Isn’t this just a cheap actionness model selecting top-K frames before AdaTAD, with some bookkeeping to avoid leakage?”

要反驳这个攻击，你必须在文本里强化四个不可替代点：

1. **TAD-specific utility is not actionness.**
   Actionness 表示“像不像动作”，但 TAD utility 还包括 boundary、short action、ranking suppression、duplicate avoidance。你现在写了这个观点，但没有 formal target 和 diagnostics。

2. **TAD sparse acquisition must preserve metric temporal geometry.**
   Classification/VideoQA frame selection可以把时间当集合；TAD 必须输出 start/end time。你已经写了这个差异，这是最强叙事点之一，但 Method 必须证明 detector 真的在 original-time axis 上 assignment/decode/NMS。

3. **Deploy-visible selection is part of the method, not implementation hygiene.**
   No GT/no teacher/no raw prediction/no cache ledger 是强点。它能区分你的方法和 oracle/teacher-guided evaluation leakage，但要写成 formal protocol + validator，而不是只写 flags。

4. **Dynamic budget must be calibrated, not just variable K.**
   如果动态 K 没有 global compute constraint 和 matched-average-K control，它不是创新，只是不公平多看。你已经意识到 dynamic budget can hide compute changes，但 skeleton 还没把控制实验写完整。

与 ActionFormer/AdaTAD 的关系也必须更准确：
你不是替代 ActionFormer/AdaTAD；你是在它们之前加入 deployable acquisition，并在 detector 端加入 original-time sparse coordinate interface。AdaTAD/OpenTAD 代表的是强 dense/end-to-end TAD stack，而你解决的是 expensive dense evidence delivery。OpenTAD 作为统一框架和公平比较背景也应被纳入 Related Work，而不是只作为隐含代码基座。([arXiv][1])

与 adaptive video computation 的区别：
最近 long-video frame selection work 通常解决 QA/reasoning/VLM token budget，目标是选出回答问题所需 key frames，而不是回归 action segment 边界。FFS 和 AKS 这类工作可以作为对比：它们证明 frame selection 是活跃方向，但也凸显你的 TAD 版本必须处理 temporal metric geometry。([CVF开放获取][2])

### 9.2 Abstract 的 CVPR 风格改写版本

Temporal action detection requires not only recognizing actions in long untrimmed videos, but also preserving temporal evidence needed for accurate start and end localization. Uniformly reducing the input length lowers computation, yet it allocates the same budget to background, action interiors, short actions, and boundary regions, and it can silently change the temporal coordinate system seen by the detector. We introduce DUCA-TAD, a deployable sparse acquisition framework for budgeted temporal action detection. A low-cost temporal browser predicts acquisition scores from deploy-visible signals such as actionness, temporal change, boundary proximity, uncertainty, and redundancy proxies. A constrained ledger decoder converts these scores into an ordered set of selected dense-time positions under fixed or dynamic budget constraints. The selected observations are passed to an AdaTAD/ActionFormer-style detector through an original-time sparse grid, so assignment, decoding, and post-processing remain expressed in the dense video time axis rather than selected-frame rank. During training, detector-derived utility targets can supervise the browser, while validation and test ledgers contain only selected indices, budget metadata, provenance hashes, and no-leakage flags. We evaluate DUCA-TAD with matched-budget sparse acquisition baselines, high-tIoU localization metrics, compute accounting, and ledger diagnostics that separate actionness selection, boundary-sensitive utility, dynamic budgeting, and temporal-coordinate preservation.

最终版有真实结果后，最后一句必须换成定量句，例如：
“On THUMOS14, DUCA-TAD reduces backbone observations by X% while matching/improving dense/uniform AdaTAD by Y average mAP and Z mAP@0.7.”

### 9.3 Introduction 最后一段贡献列表改写版本

This work makes three contributions.

1. **We formulate deployable sparse acquisition for TAD.** Unlike video classification or QA frame selection, the selector must operate before the expensive detector, use only deployment-visible signals at validation and test time, and preserve original temporal coordinates for segment localization.

2. **We propose DUCA-TAD, a detector-utility-guided acquisition method.** A lightweight temporal browser predicts boundary-sensitive acquisition value from cheap temporal signals, and a constrained decoder produces an auditable sparse ledger under fixed or dynamic budget constraints.

3. **We introduce an original-time sparse detector interface and evaluation protocol.** The ledger stores selected dense indices and provenance metadata, enabling AdaTAD/ActionFormer-style detectors to consume sparse observations while decoding predictions in the original time axis. The evaluation separates uniform, random, actionness-only, boundary-aware, utility-guided, and dynamic-budget policies under matched compute and no-leakage audits.

---

### 9.4 Method opening paragraph 改写版本

DUCA-TAD consists of four deterministic stages at inference time and one training-only supervision path. Given a dense candidate window, a lightweight temporal browser computes deploy-visible descriptors and predicts per-position acquisition scores. A constrained ledger decoder selects an ordered subset of dense-time indices under a fixed or budget-predicted count while enforcing validity, duplicate, and maximum-gap constraints. The detector loader gathers only the selected observations, but attaches their original dense coordinates and support cells as a sparse temporal grid. Ledger-aware detector operators then perform assignment, decoding, and post-processing in the original time axis. During acquisition training, detector-derived utility targets supervise the browser on the training split; these targets, teacher outputs, raw detector predictions, and caches are never serialized into validation or test ledgers.
