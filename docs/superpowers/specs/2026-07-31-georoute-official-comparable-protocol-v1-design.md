# GeoRoute official-comparable protocol v1

Date: 2026-07-31
Status: implemented locally; remote preflight not yet executed
Scientific verdict: `NO_NEW_PRO_DISCUSSION_REQUIRED / PREFLIGHT_BEFORE_F1`

## Research question

Does detector-supervised, ROI-free exact-K native-token selection improve
high-tIoU temporal localization over deterministic and random exact-K controls,
while reducing matched native-source computation relative to dense execution?
Only after that claim survives may geometry be introduced as a conditional
extension.

This protocol is deliberately split into development selection and a later
single sealed official evaluation. Development numbers are not paper-table
results.

## Evidence lineage

The immediate parent is the no-performance DDP FP16-cast repair gate at runtime
source `685f935e759d5d78f94e5f208997644e07bf4654`. Its sealed decision
`DDP_FP16_CAST_REPAIR_GATE_PASS_MATCHED_FORMAL_PROTOCOL_FREEZE_AUTHORIZED`
supports exactly one intervention for the matched native family:
`solver.fp16_compress=false`. It does not establish accuracy, efficiency, an
official reproduction, or a paper claim.

The immutable external anchor is the AdaTAD/OpenTAD release source
`01c58b9f2370e914150cf94d392208a4e211c053`. Its THUMOS VideoMAE-S adapter
configuration has SHA-256
`5521b6ce28cc6770e662d3dfdd4621479bc228be6131e300a92285fb4961a49c` and
reports 83.90/79.01/72.38/61.57/48.27 mAP at tIoU 0.3--0.7, average 69.03.
Those published values are a target anchor, not evidence generated by this
project.

## Correct batch semantics

OpenTAD treats `solver.{train,val,test}.batch_size` as a job-global value and
divides it by `world_size` in the dataloader. Therefore the official
`batch_size=2` recipe under two ranks is:

- config/global batch: 2;
- world size: 2;
- local batch per rank: 1.

It is not per-rank batch 2/global batch 4. The formal binding validates all
three values. The preceding single-rank real-data preflight deliberately uses
batch 2 as a stronger memory/numerical stress and labels itself
`single_rank_resource_stress_not_exact_world2_recipe=true`; it cannot be called
an official training reproduction.

## F0: no-performance admission preflight

F0 has three parallel leaves and one after-any finalizer:

1. residual straight-through, 32 real batches, single rank, stress batch 2;
2. residual ordered Plackett--Luce, identical data contract;
3. a two-rank NCCL KAT proving that default FP32 DDP reduction preserves a
   finite reduced scaled gradient of 80,000 while a detached FP16 shadow cast
   overflows.

Both real-data leaves use the default dynamic `GradScaler`, official
warmup/max-epoch hyperparameters (5/100), official scheduler/EMA transition
cadence, no retry/replay, no communication compression, and disjoint seed
2311. They emit no checkpoint, prediction, metric, evaluator, or test artifact.
Only all three PASS receipts authorize F1.

## F1: frozen development selection

F1 uses only a Fit/Gate partition of THUMOS `training`; no official validation
or test population is opened.

Frozen cells:

- arms: native-source dense, fixed lattice K=64, random K=64, residual ST
  K=64, residual ordered-PL K=64;
- seeds: 3407, 3408, 3409;
- 15 independent Slurm leaves, submitted together, plus one after-any
  finalizer;
- 60 epochs, official scheduler 5/100, global batch 2 over two ranks, AdamW,
  AMP, EMA, static graph, no DDP FP16 compression;
- source-native 180x320 input, floor 11x20 support (220 native tokens), one
  heavy VideoMAE forward;
- final-epoch EMA checkpoint only, with atomic checkpoint/sidecar binding;
- complete Gate evaluation with the project evaluator/NMS, no raw-prediction
  cache and no partial inference.

The primary accuracy score is mean(mAP@0.6, mAP@0.7). Each selector must beat
both fixed and random for every paired seed and have lower matched
model-plus-postprocess p50 latency than native dense for every seed. ST versus
PL can be selected only by strict paired-seed accuracy/cost Pareto dominance:
non-inferior on both axes for every seed and strictly better on both mean axes.
Any ambiguity produces `HOLD_NO_OFFICIAL_TEST`. Geometry is excluded from F1.

F1 profiles and route telemetry are development diagnostics. They support only
method selection; they do not support an end-to-end efficiency or paper claim.

## F2: later sealed official evaluation

F2 is not opened by F0 or F1. A separate immutable certificate must freeze:

1. the exact upstream released-checkpoint evaluator anchor;
2. an exact upstream-source training reproduction;
3. a current-source official-recipe bridge;
4. the no-compression bridge;
5. matched native-source dense, fixed/random controls, and the frozen selector;
6. at least three preregistered seeds;
7. identical split/windows/padding, updates, optimizer/scheduler, AMP/EMA,
   checkpoint rule, evaluator and NMS;
8. selector-inclusive decode-to-NMS latency, peak memory, energy and actual
   token/route cost.

Development is followed by one sealed official-test opening. Released-checkpoint
evaluation must match every published field within 0.05 percentage points.
New official-source training must be within 1.0 point average and 1.5 points
per tIoU before GeoRoute rows can enter a paper table.

## Failure and claim rules

- No resume, partial checkpoint, missing arm, single-arm replacement or
  post-result threshold change.
- A failed/incomplete matrix emits no cross-cell performance inference.
- A complete but ambiguous matrix is a scientific HOLD, not an engineering
  failure.
- F0 and F1 cannot authorize “Geometry Zoom”, official efficiency,
  `paper_ready`, or a publication claim.
- The only positive F1 outcome is authorization to implement the sealed F2
  protocol; it still does not open the test set.
